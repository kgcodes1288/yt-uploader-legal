Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

"YT Uploader" is a personal, single-user tool that uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

The app accesses only the Google account of its owner, and only with the owner's explicit consent, to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and metadata.

Data storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored on the owner's private computer. No data is collected from other users, sold, shared, or sent to any third party. No analytics or advertising are used.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
